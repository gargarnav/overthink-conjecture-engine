# Overthink Conjecture Engine

A falsification-first discovery loop for combinatorial game theory: exact game data, classical and AI conjecture proposers, falsification on at least 10× more data, Lean 4 proofs of survivors, and an open conjecture registry that keeps every refuted conjecture.

- **Code, datasets and experiments:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/gargarnav/overthink-conjecture-engine)
- **How claims are made and checked:** [Methodology](methodology.md)
- **What has actually worked and failed, dated:** [Results log](results_log.md)
- **Current numbers:** [Metrics](metrics.md) · [Costs](costs.md)
- **The one result that may be new:** [P-positions of W({a,a+1}) for every a](results/W_pair_theorem.md)
